# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players who want Robux delivered via their Roblox username, and the shop owner who manually verifies payment receipts and fulfils orders.

## Product Purpose

Sealy Blox Shop sells fixed Robux packages through a username-only order flow. Customers pay by DuitNow/Touch 'n Go QR, upload a receipt, and use a short order code to check status.

## Positioning

Username-only delivery with manual receipt approval: customers never need to hand over a Roblox password.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Packages are 100R to 1000R at RM3.85 per 100R. Customer phone number and receipt are required. Roblox profile lookup needs a Netlify serverless proxy. The administrator needs order status changes and package stock controls. Firebase is the intended data/auth/storage backend but has not yet been connected.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Sealy Blox Shop. Supplied assets: blue ribbon pricing card and Touch 'n Go/DuitNow QR owned by Betty Gregory. Copy should be straightforward and safety-first.

## Evidence on Hand

Pricing card and payment QR are in the user-supplied images. No testimonials, sales figures, or delivery-time claims are provided.

## Product Principles

- Make the selected Robux package unmistakable.
- Make payment and receipt submission easy to verify.
- Show a customer exactly which Roblox profile receives the order.
- Give the operator a clear review queue.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, clear status text in addition to color, and mobile-first checkout.
